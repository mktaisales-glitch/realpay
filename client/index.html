How it works

4 steps
  1. Agree the milestones. Each has a price and a checklist of what the proof must show.
  2. Fund with PayPal. Materials go to the supplier now. The labour is only authorized and held.
  3. AI checks the proof. Photo or certificate against the checklist. Reused or edited files are flagged.
  4. Money moves on proof. Small, clear stages release on their own. The rest waits for your OK.

Why: paying everything up front puts all the risk on the homeowner, paying at the end puts it on the contractor. Banks release construction loans in stages, against proof. RealPay brings that to everyday jobs.
