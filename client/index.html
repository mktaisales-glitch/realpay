Pay for the work, not the promise.

RealPay splits a job into milestones. Materials are paid up front, the labour is only authorized with PayPal, and it is charged only when AI has checked photo or document proof that the work is done.

The problem we're solving

“I paid €3,000 up front for a renovation. The work never got done.”

Paying everything up front puts all the risk on the homeowner. Paying everything at the end puts it on the contractor. Banks solved this for construction loans decades ago: release money in stages, against proof. RealPay brings that to everyday jobs.

How it works

1

Agree the milestones

Pick a template. Each stage has a price, dates and a checklist of what the proof must show.

2

Fund with PayPal

The supplier's share is paid now. The labour is authorized and held. Pay Later is offered when you're eligible.

3

AI checks the proof

The contractor uploads a photo or certificate. AI compares it with the checklist and flags reused or edited files.

4

Money moves on proof

Small, clear stages release automatically. Everything else waits for your OK. A 5% retention covers snagging.
